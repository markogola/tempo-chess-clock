Who we are

Tempo: Chess Clock ("Tempo", "the app") is a two-player chess clock for Android phones and tablets, Fire tablets and Fire TV. It is published by Odhuno App Developers ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app, and it applies to the app wherever you download it, including Google Play and the Amazon Appstore.

What the app itself stores

Tempo does not ask you to create an account and never asks for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.

Your preferences (the time control you chose, which side plays White, and whether sound and vibration are on) are stored only on your device. We never receive them, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app. The app does not read your contacts, photos, files, location or microphone.

The app requests one permission, vibrate, which is used only for the optional move and time-out vibrations.

Advertising

Tempo is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. Ads appear on the time-control screen and, at most once every few minutes, after a finished game. No ads are shown while a game is being played.

The app store you download Tempo from (Google Play or the Amazon Appstore) may also collect information under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Where the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent message before ads use your data. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Time control > Ad privacy choices.
  • Personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (on Android, Settings > Privacy > Ads; on Fire devices, Settings > Privacy or Preferences > Privacy Settings).
  • Deleting your data. Uninstalling Tempo removes everything the app stored on your device. For data held by Google, use the controls in your Google account or contact Google.
  • Your rights. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because the app does not hold personal information about you, most requests are best directed to Google; we are happy to help at the address below.

Children

Tempo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
