Made for the board

Everything a clock needs, nothing it doesn't

Set it beside the board and play. No account, no setup, no distractions during the game.

White and Black, at a glance

An ivory clock for White and a charcoal one for Black. The running clock glows amber; the other steps back.

Instant response

Your move ends the moment your finger touches the screen, so close games are decided on the board.

Every time control

15 presets from bullet to classical, or build your own with custom minutes, seconds and increment.

Fischer, Bronstein and delay

All three increment styles used in clubs and tournaments, timed to a tenth of a second.

Low-time warning

In the last ten seconds the clock turns red and shows tenths, with a clear time-out and winner display.

Phone, tablet and Fire TV

On a TV the clocks sit side by side for the whole room, and the remote's OK button ends each move.
